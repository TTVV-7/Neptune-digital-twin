Each drop-in's place to about a kilometre: the cell of a grid 1 km each way that it was in, never where in it, counted a day with nothing of who. A visitor counts once a day in each cell, so these are fewer than the drop-ins above, and not people: one visitor at three places in a day counts three. A place's count over one day is its visitors; over longer, its visitor-days. A start spot's cells are one place in the table. Your own visits (this page's accounts) aren't counted. Drag to move the map; click it, then scroll to zoom (or Ctrl and scroll), or use + and −; on a phone, pinch, drag, or the buttons. The map is about 5 km squares zoomed in: a place's name in the table shows it on the map, and Drive opens the game there, street by street.

Busy drop-in places for the rotation

The rotation goes round the game's own places (the start spots, the places' pages and the Moon's places) and the busy drop-in places approved here, a new order each time round, one a period; a period's challenge is fixed the first time anyone asks for it, so nothing changed here changes a live one, unless you change that one, which starts a new board (the old one is kept apart). Busy places are where people drop in that aren't the game's own: anyone with many addresses can push one up the list, so none goes into the rotation without your approval. Your own runs (this page's accounts) are taken but kept off the boards, as you know each place and kind before it's live, and choose them. Back to the rotation on a live one the rotation chose leaves it as it is (it's the rotation's already).

Counts only, nothing of who. Days are UTC, and each period ends with today so far. ▲ and ▼ compare with the as many days just before it. Referrers, countries and page speed in more detail are in Cloudflare's dashboard (Web Analytics).
