Controls

ClickCapture mouse to look around (Esc releases)
W A S D / arrowsMove
ShiftRun (walk) / boost (fly)
FSwitch between walking and flying
SpaceJump (walk) / up (fly)
E / QUp / down (fly)
Right-dragLook around without capturing the mouse
LLibrary: place vehicles, trailers, bins, barriers...
Click itemSelect it; drag to move it
R / Shift+RRotate the selected or placing item
DeleteRemove the selected item
EscCancel placing / deselect
VDrive the selected or nearest vehicle or machine (race mode); V again to get out
Space (excavator)Hold to dig; Q / E swing, R / F reach, X dump side
PPlacement panel: exact positions
HShow / hide this help
