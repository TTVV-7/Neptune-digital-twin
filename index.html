Click to look around · WASD move · wheel zooms · F walk / fly · V drive · H help

Controls

The Controls panel (bottom right) shows the keys for what you are doing right now, and the Show panel above it what is drawn. H shows or hides this list.

Moving

WASD or arrowsMove
ShiftRun (walking) / faster (flying)
FSwitch between walking and flying
SpaceJump (walking) / up (flying)
E / QUp / down (flying); C also goes down
WheelZoom toward the point under the cursor, or away from it; Shift for bigger steps. Or pinch a trackpad
GGhost: go through walls, to look inside; again to bump into them
TTurn the view along walls you hit: on / off

The higher you fly, the faster: from 1 km up you cross the terminal in seconds.

Looking

Click the mapCapture the mouse to look around; Esc frees it
Right-dragLook around without capturing the mouse

Building

LOpen or close the library
Click a cardThen click the ground to place it; Shift+click places another
Drag a cardDrop it on the map to place it there
R or WheelTurn the item you are placing
ToolsLaydown area, Excavation, Stockpile, Flatten area: drag a rectangle on the ground, or click for the usual size
Click an itemSelect it; drag it to move it
Round handleDrag it to rotate the selected item; Shift snaps to 15°
Square handlesOn an area, excavation or stockpile: drag to resize; Shift for any size
Arrow in a pitDrag down to dig deeper, up for shallower
ToolbarThe buttons over the selected item: rotate, size (type it in), drive, edit, hide, delete
R / Shift+RTurn the selected item 15°
DeleteRemove the selected item (Undo for 5 seconds)
EscCancel placing or drawing, or deselect
PPlacement panel: exact position, heading and size

Driving

VDrive the selected or nearest (25 m) vehicle or machine, or a new pickup
W / SGas / brake; hold S to reverse
A / DSteer
ShiftBoost
SpaceHop
CChase or bonnet camera
WheelChase camera nearer or farther
V or EscGet out; it parks where it stopped

Excavator

Space or hold the mouse buttonDig
Q / ESwing
R / F or WheelReach
XDump side
WASDDrive the tracks
COperator camera
V or EscGet out

Showing

BBefore / after: the site as Google captured it (nothing flattened, dug, piled or placed), or with your changes
Show panelAbove the Controls (the eye when folded): hide flatten areas, excavations, stockpiles, laydown areas, shoring, labels or a whole category such as Vehicles
HideOn the selected item's toolbar: hide just that one. The Show panel lists it to show again

Other

HShow or hide this help
Drop a fileDrop a .glb file anywhere on the page to load your own model
